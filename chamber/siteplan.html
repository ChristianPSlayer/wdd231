Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide useful information for people who use electric bicycles for food delivery services. The website will include recommendations for e-bike equipment, safety tips, battery maintenance, and practical advice to help delivery riders work more efficiently and safely.

Scenarios

  • What equipment should I buy before starting food delivery with an e-bike?
  • How can I extend my e-bike battery life during long delivery shifts?
